Colors & Gradients

Stats: white→#f0ebff gradient pills

Depth & Shadows
Multi-layer shadows (near + far) on every card
Inset top-border highlight (1px white) for lift effect
Recording button: outer glow ring + deep shadow
Icon thumbnails: colored shadows matching their gradient
Header: directional gradient + shadow below
Layout & Density
Everything visible on one screen — no scroll required
Stats row on home + recent item = full context immediately
Recorder: badge + timer + waveform + controls + timeline all fit
Left-border accent on timeline segments (color-coded)
Section labels in small-caps for clear hierarchy
